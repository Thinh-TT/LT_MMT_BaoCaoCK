Lap trinh mang can ban - Bao cao cuoi ky
Chu de: Luong byte (Byte Stream) va Luong ky tu (Character Stream) trong Java.

1. Nguyen ly hoat dong:
- Luong byte (Byte Stream): Xu ly du lieu theo tung byte (8-bit binary). Cac lop tieu bieu la InputStream va OutputStream (vi du: FileInputStream, FileOutputStream). Luong nay phu hop cho moi loai file, dac biet la file nhi phan nhu hinh anh (.png, .jpg), am thanh (.mp3), video (.mp4) hoac file thuc thi (.exe).
- Luong ky tu (Character Stream): Xu ly du lieu theo tung ky tu Unicode (16-bit). Cac lop tieu bieu la Reader va Writer (vi du: FileReader, FileWriter). Luong nay tu dong ma hoa va giai ma theo bang ma (Charset, mac dinh la UTF-8), rat phu hop cho file van ban text (.txt, .json, .csv).
